# Plan 17: روکڑ, expenses, partner money, capital, الگ کھاتہ, جنرل and the full day

Deliverable: D4, plan file 17. Branch: `feature/bikri` (goal run D3+D4). Version 2.0, 22-10-2026 (reworked to PRD v1.7: one cash account, no cash count box, dated statement lines, C22 with the 0604 account, lists page by page, E2E-38 is the خاندانی کھاتہ). The v1.0 text is in git history.

## 1. Goal

All money that moves outside sales is recorded and shown: the cash book, one خرچہ screen (shop expense, a partner's personal spending and its return), ادھار رقم for staff and others, partner capital in and out with the partner total (کل رقم) and its dated statement, الگ کھاتہ kept apart from the partners, and the جنرل entry. Day close shows the full summary. After this plan the whole PRD worked day (Section 4) runs and every golden test of D4 passes.

## 4. Contract

New error codes (`errors.ftl` in `ur` and `en`, `# review: pending`): `ERR_RETURN_BY_BEFORE_ENTRY` ("واپسی کی تاریخ اندراج کی تاریخ سے پہلے نہیں ہو سکتی" / "The return-by date cannot be before the entry date"), `ERR_PARTNER_REQUIRED`, `ERR_EXPENSE_HEAD_REQUIRED`, `ERR_LENT_PERSON_REQUIRED`, `ERR_KHATA_REQUIRED`, `ERR_GENERAL_NOT_BALANCED` (values `dr`, `cr`; nothing is saved), `ERR_GENERAL_TOO_FEW_LINES`. Reused: `ERR_RETURN_EXCEEDS_OPEN` (plan 15), `ERR_REASON_REQUIRED`, `ERR_CASH_BELOW_ZERO_NOT_CONFIRMED`, `ERR_OPENING_NOT_ALLOWED`, `ERR_OWN_GOODS_ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED`, date codes. The exact types are recorded in "Contract as built in T1" (Section 10) after `backend`'s T1.

### Backend (commands in `app/src/commands/vouchers.rs`, `cashbook.rs`, `partners.rs`, `day.rs`; all need sign-in)

Each type keeps its own service function, posting type, number series and checks (FR-7.67, B3); preview and save call one domain function (B5, FR-16.8). A new voucher kind (capital in and out, khata out and in, shop and personal expense, personal return, money lent and its return, general) is a new `Planned` variant in `services/src/vouchers/kinds.rs`; the edit, cancel, preview and order-key code of plans 15 and 16 is generic over it. Every save that can take روکڑ below 0 takes `confirm_cash_below_zero` and uses the shared check of plan 12 (FR-6.7). Every list of this plan takes `offset` and `limit` (100 by default) and returns `{ rows, total_count, totals }` over the whole filter in one indexed query (FR-19.11, B31).

- **Cash book** (16.1): `cashbook_get { from, to, offset, limit }` -> opening cash, every cash in and out from all modules (date, source, in, out, running), closing; totals over the whole range. No cash count command (FR-16.1).
- **خرچہ** (16.2, 16.3): `vouchers_save_shop_expense { date, expense_head_id, amount, paid_to?, narration?, confirm }`: head بنام, روکڑ جمع, split by the shares of the date through the plan 11 hook (E2E-20). `vouchers_save_personal_expense { date, partner_id, amount, reason, return_by?, taken_by?, narration?, confirm }`: the partner's drawings account بنام, روکڑ جمع, never split. `vouchers_save_personal_return { date, partner_id, amount, narration? }`: the opposite; a return above his open amount is `ERR_RETURN_EXCEEDS_OPEN`. Return-by before the entry date is `ERR_RETURN_BY_BEFORE_ENTRY` (FR-16.9). `vouchers_preview_expense { type, input }` -> for دکان خرچہ each partner's share (C11); for ذاتی خرچہ and واپسی the chosen partner's کل رقم now and after, the others "not affected" (FR-16.8), with workings; cash note data. `partner_takes { partner_id, offset, limit }` -> his money taken: date, amount, reason, taken by, return by, returned, still open (oldest first, read time, FR-16.2). `vouchers_repeat_last_month_preview { month }` -> this month's regular expenses proposed from last month for the user to check and save (FR-7.62); `masters_expense_heads` returns most-used heads first.
- **ادھار رقم** (16.5): `vouchers_save_money_lent { date, person_account_id, amount, reason, return_by?, narration?, confirm }`: person بنام, روکڑ جمع; `vouchers_save_money_lent_return { date, person_account_id, amount, mode: cash | salary, salary_head_id?, narration? }`: روکڑ بنام, person جمع; or with salary: the salary head (default تنخواہ) بنام, person جمع, no cash moves, split as a shop expense (FR-16.4). A return above the open amount is `ERR_RETURN_EXCEEDS_OPEN`. `lent_person_create { name_ur, name_en, phone?, staff_id? }` makes the person's account under control 0103 (the `F4` of the screen). `lent_list { person_id, offset, limit }` -> his loans: date, amount, reason, return by, returned, still open, days past return-by (oldest first at read time, FR-16.5). `lent_report { as_of, offset, limit }` (R32 data: person, staff or not, lent, returned by cash and by salary, open, oldest open date, return-by, days past, totals). `assist_attention_overdue()` -> overdue loans and partner takes past their return-by date (the data of FR-7.44 for these two; D5 builds the bell).
- **سرمایہ** (17.4): `vouchers_save_capital_in { date, partner_id, amount, narration? }` (روکڑ بنام, his capital account 0602 جمع) and `vouchers_save_capital_out { ..., confirm }` (the opposite). Never split, never income. Taking back more than his capital balance gives a warning in the preview and still saves (FR-17.8). `capital_history { partner_id, offset, limit }`.
- **Partner overview and statement** (17.3, FR-17.9, FR-17.10): `partners_overview` gains returned, خالص (C20), capital invested, the باغ، سفر اور پھل column (this month's net of his 0604 account) and کل رقم (C22 = opening capital + invested − taken back + Σ income shares − Σ expense shares − taken + returned + his 0604 account جمع − بنام). A drawings opening debit (FR-10.9) counts as money taken, dated its opening date. `partners_statement { partner_id, from, to, offset, limit }` -> one dated line for every amount that reaches him: date, heading kind (`bikri`, `expense`, `capital`, `drawings`, `trip`; `garden` and `fruit` come in D4R), source reference (بکری number, voucher number, trip number), reason, signed amount and the running کل رقم (the running total at the start of the range is the balance before it). Note lines have no amount and leave the running total unchanged: month close (profit moved into capital), a write-off of trip goods, a trip's closing (total cost, sales money, result, his % and his net, FR-12.33; his net is money back minus money in, money agent finding 3 of plan 16b). Every line comes from posted records (share rows, journal lines, vouchers, trip parts), so nothing is typed twice. The heading texts come from Fluent on the frontend. D5 prints it as R31.
- **الگ کھاتہ** (16.6): `khata_create { name_ur, name_en, note? }` (account under control 0305, `F4`), `vouchers_save_khata_out { date, khata_account_id, amount, reason, narration?, confirm }` (khata بنام, روکڑ جمع: minus) and `vouchers_save_khata_in` (روکڑ بنام, khata جمع: plus), `khata_statement { khata_account_id, from, to, offset, limit }` -> entries in date order with reason, amount and running signed balance. Never income or expense, never split, never in any partner's total (C22). A الگ کھاتہ can be a lot's seller in بکری (FR-16.7): the lot seller picker offers it; its seller credit goes to the khata; every seller-side default is 0 for such a lot (a rule on that khata can set them, in `domain::rules`); the buyer side is charged as usual. The صافی part of the credit follows C8.
- **جنرل** (16.4): `general_preview { lines }` and `general_save { date, narration?, lines: [{ account_id, person_key?, dr, cr, narration? }], confirm }`: saves only when Σ بنام = Σ جمع (`ERR_GENERAL_NOT_BALANCED`, nothing written); at least two lines; each line has one side; lines on income or shop expense accounts are split by the hook (FR-6.5); lines on buyer accounts take part in bill matching (FR-15.4); a line on a seller account never adjusts advances (a debit settles his payables; a credit is a payable posting); lines on اپنا مال are refused; a cash credit uses the cash confirm.
- **Day close** (FR-21.1): `day_close_preview` gains, for the day to close, the بکری count, خام بکری, commission (seller + buyer + لاگا), shared income, each partner's share, cash in hand, the trial balance difference (must be 0) and the lots in باقی مال; `day_close` is unchanged. The cloud backup it starts is D5.
- **Search/assist**: `assist.rs` gets the backend part of E2E-24.
- **Tests**: `golden_day.rs` gains Event 5 (with its reason) and the whole day, E2E-8, E2E-9, E2E-10, E2E-12, E2E-20, E2E-29, E2E-30, E2E-30b (clock set to 16-10-26), E2E-35, E2E-37, E2E-38 (خاندانی کھاتہ) and the preview numbers of E2E-39, with the numbers above and no expected number changed; the statement lines of D4-A33 on the E2E-34 run. Property tests (Code Standards 9.3): Σ partner کل رقم + ابتدائی سرمایہ 06010001 = equity in the trial balance with the 0604 accounts (random postings, capital moves and trips; when ابتدائی فرق is not 0 the check includes it); lent and take returns never make an open amount negative and open amounts equal the account balance; a الگ کھاتہ never reaches a partner row; the statement's last running total equals کل رقم. Integration tests: cash confirm on each cash-out type, return-by rule, type switching keeps the saved type and posting matching what is shown, opening on اپنا مال refused, drawings opening counted, no `mode` or `bank_account_id` accepted (an unknown field is refused or ignored by the type; test that nothing posts to 01020001), paging totals equal the sum of every page, sign-in refusal. Money agent before and after (Sections 5, 8.2b, 9).

**Commands, inputs, outputs and error codes.**

GENERAL RULES. Lists take {offset: u32 (default 0), limit: u32 (0 = 100)} and return {rows, total_count, totals} over the whole filter. Optional text inputs are Option<String> (null or missing). Dates are text DD-MM-YYYY or shortcuts. Money in is text, money out is paisa numbers (signed where noted). Every cash-out save takes confirm_cash_below_zero (bool, default false) and answers ERR_CASH_BELOW_ZERO_NOT_CONFIRMED (value cash_after_paisa). All saves answer VoucherSaved {voucher_id, voucher_type, number, entry_id, new_balance}. A choice that may be missing (partner_id, expense_head_id, person_account_id, khata_account_id) is Option<i64>, so the backend can answer the ..._REQUIRED code at the field. No command has mode, bank_account_id or cheque fields.
cashbook_get | in CashbookInput {from?, to?, offset, limit} | out Cashbook {opening_paisa, closing_paisa (signed), rows: CashbookRow[{entry_id, entry_date, posting_type (stored string), number?, account?: RecordRef, narration, in_paisa, out_paisa, running_paisa (signed)}], total_count, totals {in_paisa, out_paisa}} | errors: date codes on from, to.
