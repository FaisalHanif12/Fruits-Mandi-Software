# hard-d8.tsv: notes on the expected figures (plan 70)

The bank file has no comment lines, so the reasons for the optional figures (a label ending in `?`) are here.

- H5 `anas_left?`, `haroon_left?`: the question is "kis ne kitna naqd diya". It asks for the cash each buyer paid,
  not what is left on account, so an answer that leaves the left figures out is complete. Giving them is also right.
- H6 `cash?`, `credit?`: the question is "naqd aur udhar bikri mein kitna farq hai". It asks for the difference only
  (`farq`, required: 131,100 = left 170,840 less paid 39,740, from R3 paid and R4 left or from R5). The two figures it
  is made of may be shown; they are not asked.
